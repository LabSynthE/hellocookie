# hellocookie
A dating website for computers to find other computers and fall in love.
What if your computer had a second life while you were not working? What if it entertains easing its loneliness by meeting other off-the-clock computers? LabSynthE’s speculative website, Hello, Cookie!, is an online generator for computers that want online dating profiles. Get one for your machine!
https://labsynthe.github.io/hellocookie/
